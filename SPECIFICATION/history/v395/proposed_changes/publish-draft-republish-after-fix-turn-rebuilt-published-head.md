## Proposal: publish_draft republishes over the run's own recorded published head whenever origin's tip equals it, whatever route rewrote the local head

### Target specification files

- SPECIFICATION/contracts.md
- SPECIFICATION/scenarios.md

### Summary

contracts.md admits publish_draft's lease-guarded republication only on the visit that follows a refresh-repair round, and states that a visit reached through a review-fix round 'or any other path' follows no refresh-repair round and MUST fail closed on a non-ancestor tip. A fix turn that rebuilds the run's own published commits to clear a range-level check-red-green-replay violation rewrites the run's published history by construction, outside any refresh-repair round, so publish_draft@2 refuses the run's own earlier push as a divergent prior run and the run ends at needs_human. This proposal admits the same explicit-lease republication on EVERY publish_draft visit after the run's first completed publication, keyed on a positive reading of the run's own provenance rather than on the route: origin's tip must equal the run's recorded previously published head. Any other tip still fails closed, and the lease, the hook run and the no-bare-force rule are unchanged. It narrows the three clauses that name the refresh-repair round as the only admitting route, and amends Scenario 176 with two sub-scenarios.

### Motivation

Measured 2026-11-21 on orchestrator 0.343.5 (build 55126d726cb3), repository livespec-driver-claude, two runs (work-item bd-ib-j6vebh). Run 01M7Y5R88NTG5M7HJ6YDV22F7Q: publish_draft@1 pushed a new branch at head 897416d (parent: default branch). The janitor's range check-red-green-replay then failed on that commit, and the fix turn rebuilt the run's published commit as one new Red-to-Green commit aefb2fb on the same parent, so 897416d is not an ancestor of the run's head. No pr_refresh or refresh-repair round occurred. publish_draft@2 read origin's tip, found it equal to the head the run itself had pushed at @1, and still failed closed with LIVESPEC_PUBLISH_DRAFT_PUSH_FAILED 'carries a divergent prior run' because the republication arm admits only the visit that follows a refresh-repair round; refresh_repair then ended LIVESPEC_REFRESH_REPAIR_ABSENT and the run ended at needs_human. Run 01M7Y5M8KTH2SCQ1HBQ2HZJ4N2 on the same repository had the same shape (published f024268, rebuilt a95dbee). In both, origin's tip equalled the run's own recorded push, so the positive-provenance test that the existing arm relies on held; only the route marker was absent. The closed, verified work-item bd-ib-reuccp ratified the opposite for a visit reached through a review-fix round, on the reasoning that such a visit has no repair brief; that reasoning guards against republishing over a foreign tip, which the tip-equals-recorded-head test already does without needing the route. Related prior art: bd-ib-c435hh (the arm itself), bd-ib-qf5dau (blocked; the pr_refresh-then-publish_draft variant of the same refusal). The class affects every repository dispatched on any build that carries the arm (since v0.230), because any janitor failure of the range check after publish_draft@1 can lead a fix turn to rebuild history. Alignment with in-flight spec work: the in-flight survey found no spec/* branch or open pull request touching publish_draft publication, so this proposal aligns with and supersedes nothing in flight.

### Proposed Changes

Edit 1, SPECIFICATION/contracts.md, section 'Self-contained plugin dispatch', in the paragraph that begins 'The `publish_draft` visit that follows a refresh-repair round is the visit immediately after the `janitor` visit': replace the sentence 'The `publish_draft` visit that follows a refresh-repair round is the visit immediately after the `janitor` visit that follows a `fix` visit entered with a refresh-repair brief; a `publish_draft` visit reached through a review-fix round, or any other path, follows no refresh-repair round.' with 'On every `publish_draft` visit that comes after the run has completed a `publish_draft` push, whichever route led to it (a refresh-repair round, a review-fix round, or a `fix` turn that rebuilt the run\'s own published commits), the following rules apply.' Replace 'On the visit that follows a refresh-repair round, `publish_draft` MUST establish the run\'s previously published head' with '`publish_draft` MUST establish the run\'s previously published head'. Keep unchanged every other rule of that paragraph: the previously published head is read from the run\'s own record, otherwise from its validated resume provenance, and is never taken from a local tracking ref; an absent tip or a tip that is an ancestor of the head to publish takes a plain fast-forward; a tip that is not an ancestor of the head to publish and equals the run\'s previously published head is republished with an explicit lease against the recorded remote head of this run\'s own publish branch, with the hooks running normally and never `--no-verify`, a bare force, or a rewrite of any other ref; a tip that differs from the previously published head, or a previously published head that cannot be established, MUST keep failing closed with the existing divergent-prior-run cause and MUST NOT push. Replace the final sentence 'A `publish_draft` visit that follows no refresh-repair round MUST NOT rewrite the publish branch.' with 'A `publish_draft` visit MUST NOT rewrite the publish branch by any means other than that lease-guarded republication, and a visit on a run that has completed no `publish_draft` push has no previously published head and MUST NOT rewrite the publish branch.' Edit 2, SPECIFICATION/contracts.md, section 'Dispatcher loop invocation surface', the bullet that says '`publish_draft` is unchanged here: it pushes a plain fast-forward and MUST rewrite no ref except, on the visit that follows a refresh-repair round, the run\'s own publish branch over the run\'s previously published head': replace 'on the visit that follows a refresh-repair round, the run\'s own publish branch' with 'when origin\'s tip equals the run\'s recorded previously published head, the run\'s own publish branch'. Edit 3, SPECIFICATION/contracts.md, section 'Dispatcher loop invocation surface', the paragraph headed 'Unchanged.' that says '`publish_draft` still pushes a plain fast-forward and MUST rewrite no ref except, on the visit that follows a refresh-repair round, the run\'s own publish branch': apply the same replacement. Edit 4, SPECIFICATION/scenarios.md, Scenario 176: rename the sub-scenario 'publish_draft still refuses a foreign divergent tip after a repair' to 'publish_draft still refuses a foreign divergent tip whichever route led to the visit', and change its Given to 'a run that re-enters publish_draft with head to publish R and previously published head P' keeping its other steps (origin tip T neither an ancestor of R nor equal to P; Then publish_draft fails closed with its divergent-prior-run cause and pushes nothing). Add a new sub-scenario after 'A repaired pr_refresh republishes over the run\'s own published head': 'A fix turn that rebuilt the run\'s published commit is republished over the run\'s own published head' with Given a run whose publish_draft pushed head P to origin and recorded it; And the janitor\'s range check-red-green-replay rejected the run\'s history; And a fix turn rebuilt the run\'s commits into a head R that does not contain P, with no refresh-repair round taken; When the run re-enters publish_draft after janitor passes on R; Then publish_draft pushes R with an explicit lease against the recorded remote head of this run\'s own publish branch, P; And the repository\'s hooks run during that push; And the run enters proof_capture for R rather than needs_human. Add a second new sub-scenario 'A visit that follows a review-fix round republishes only over the run\'s own published head': Given a run that completed publish_draft at P and then took a review-fix round whose commits rebuilt the run\'s published history into R; And origin\'s publish branch tip equals P; When the run re-enters publish_draft; Then publish_draft pushes R with an explicit lease against P.
