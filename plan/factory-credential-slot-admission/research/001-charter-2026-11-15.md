## Charter

### Scope

This plan covers the factory's session-headroom admission: the step that chooses
which numbered Claude pool slot (`CLAUDE_CODE_OAUTH_TOKEN__ANTHROPIC_N`, written
`__ANTHROPIC_N` below) a dispatch takes its setup token from. It covers three
rules that admission does not yet apply from committed configuration:

- A committed excluded-slot setting. Admission never chooses a slot that the
  setting names. Carried by bd-ib-6j32zu ("Session-headroom admission never
  chooses a Claude pool slot named in a committed excluded-slot setting, so the
  anthropic-0/1/3 exclusion survives a seat restart").
- A weekly-utilization floor. Admission skips a slot whose seven-day remaining
  headroom is below the configured weekly floor. Carried by bd-ib-eogs2t
  ("Session-headroom admission chooses a Claude pool slot at 99 percent weekly
  utilization, because only a provider-rejected weekly status skips a slot").
- Never selecting a spend-capped slot. A slot that a live provider-exhaustion
  record names is not chosen; admission fails over to the next qualifying slot or
  to the Codex ACP fallback. Carried by bd-ib-wx4yha ("Session-headroom admission
  keeps selecting a spend-capped Claude slot, and a spend-limit answer releases
  the item instead of failing over to the next slot or the Codex ACP fallback").

Out of scope: acquiring, rotating or storing the Claude accounts themselves.
That belongs to the overseer tenant's credential plans, below.

### Context: the 2026-11-15 rulings

On 2026-11-15 legacy-coordinator-agent ruled the following for the family
factory pool:

- The pool is narrowed to exclude anthropic-0, anthropic-1 and anthropic-3.
- The pool is also narrowed to exclude any slot below caam's 10% weekly reserve.

Today that narrowing is applied by a seat-local wrapper (the `slotnarrow`
wrapper under `/var/tmp/ldseat`). It lives outside version control, so it does
not survive a seat restart, a host move or a second dispatcher. This plan makes
the narrowing committed: the exclusion set and the weekly floor become
committed dispatcher configuration that admission reads, and the wrapper is
retired.
